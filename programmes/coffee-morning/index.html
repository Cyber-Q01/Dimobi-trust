Our Work

How Family Support works

For parents coping with the added stress of autism, this is especially true. In respite care, another caregiver takes over temporarily – giving you a break for a few hours, days, or even weeks.

Support Groups“Care for Caring” Parents Programme – Mothers’ Time Out Camp – Dads’ Outings – Siblings’ Outings – Siblings’ Groups – Consultations – Workshops for Parents

Often in our Parents’ Forum various stakeholders are invited to the meeting for shared learning and lessons learnt through our interactive peer support groups.

Counselling

  • Parents and carers of children with special needs
  • Siblings, grandparents and the wider family
  • Anyone who feels isolated by their caring role
