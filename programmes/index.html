What we do

What We Do

Dimobi Childrens Disability trust offers a range of services to provide support and assistance to family members, carers, and professionals working with individuals with autism.

Our Work

What we Believe

We work closely with families, for families. Everything we do is carried out in the hope of improving the lives of our families. We achieve this by working directly with parents and carers. We are driven by what they tell us.

Autism usually manifests in the first year of life. Its onset is usually no later than 3 years.

Autism Support

These include: – Information and Helpline Services – Autism Advice Programmes – Support Groups“Care for Caring” Parents Programme – Mothers’ Time Out Camp – Dads’ Outings – Siblings’ Outings – Siblings’ Groups – Consultations – Workshops for Parents

Children's Activities

Happy Me is special needs activities using football as a vehicle to teach life skills to our children and young people, their siblings, and parents alike.

Parents' Forum

Dimobi Parents’ Forum is a growing peer-to-peer support network of many parents and carers of children and young people with learning disabilities and special education needs.

Family Support

Every parent needs break now and again. For parents coping with the added stress of autism, this is especially true. In respite care, another caregiver takes over temporarily – giving you a break for a few hours, days, or even weeks.

Workshops for Parents

Examples of parent workshops include: – Fighting Denial: Denial is a major problem faced by most parents in the community. It can result in setbacks for all children with disabilities.

Food Parcels
