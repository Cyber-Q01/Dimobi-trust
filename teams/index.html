What we Believe

We work closely with families, for families. Everything we do is carried out in the hope of improving the lives of our families.

  • We are ambitious.

    That’s why we won’t stop until we have achieved our mission. We are driven to fight and advocate for what is right.

  • We transform lives.

    We drive innovation and change wherever it will benefit families with disabled children.

  • We challenge disadvantage.

    We tackle inequality wherever it exists, and we help our families to deal with the barriers they face on a daily basis.

  • We trust, and are trusted.

    We work collaboratively to achieve more, and respect the strength and knowledge of others.

Help us give every child the chance they deserve.
