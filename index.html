Who We Are

An Award Winning Charity. A Powerful Voice for Every Child and Family.

An award-winning Manchester charity supporting children and young people up to the age of 25 with autism, learning disabilities, special educational needs and complex needs, alongside the families who care for them.

We believe disability should never define a child's potential.

We work with families, for families. We listen to parents and carers, understand the challenges they face and turn their experiences into meaningful support and action.

From advocacy and practical family support to inclusive activities, education support and opportunities for children and young people, everything we do has one purpose: to help every child feel valued, included and empowered to thrive.

We don't just support families — we stand beside them, speak up with them and walk the journey with them.

Our Mission

To empower children and young people with disabilities and autism to reach their full potential, while giving parents and carers the knowledge, confidence, practical support and powerful voice they need to build a brighter future for their families.

Our Aims

We aim to equip, empower and strengthen families by giving parents and carers the skills, confidence, information and support they need to nurture their children. We work to ensure that children and young people with disabilities have the opportunity to learn, grow, make friends, participate in their communities and enjoy the happy, fulfilling childhood they deserve.

Our Promise

No child overlooked. No voice unheard. No family left to face the journey alone.

Different needs. Equal dreams. Endless potential.

Multi-Award Winning Founder

Sherita Mandongwe

Driven by lived experience, Sherita Mandongwe turned personal challenges into purpose and purpose into action. As the Founder of Dimobi Children Disability Trust, Sherita is a multi-award-winning community leader recognised for her outstanding dedication to improving the lives of children and young people with disabilities, autism and additional needs, and empowering the families who stand beside them.

Her lived experience gives her a deep understanding of the challenges families can face. It is this experience that fuels her passion to advocate, break down barriers, challenge inequality and ensure that no child or family is left behind.

Lived experience. Powerful purpose. Lasting change.

What Drives Us

Our Purpose. One Promise. No Child Left Behind.

Everything we do is driven by one belief: every child and young person with additional needs deserves to be seen, heard, valued and given the opportunity to thrive.

1

Every Child Matters

We see the child before the disability. Every child deserves dignity, respect, opportunity and the chance to reach their full potential.

2

Families Are Not Alone

We stand beside parents, carers and families through the challenges and the victories. You do not have to walk this journey alone.

3

Every Voice Deserves to Be Heard

We listen, advocate and speak up when families struggle to be heard. Children, young people and their families must have a voice in the decisions that affect their lives.

4

Inclusion Without Limits

Disability should never be a barrier to education, friendship, activities, opportunities or belonging. Every child deserves a place in their community.

5

Support That Makes a Difference

We don't just listen — we act. Through advocacy, practical support, activities and family services, we help turn challenges into opportunities and hope into progress.

Our Values. One Unbreakable Promise.

We will never stop standing up for children and young people with disabilities and their families.

No family should struggle alone. No child should be left behind.

We are here to listen. We are here to support. We are here to walk beside you.

What we Believe

Our Values. Our Strength Our Commitment.

We believe every child and young person deserves the opportunity to be seen, heard, included and empowered to thrive.

We are ambitious for our families because they deserve nothing less. We challenge barriers, champion inclusion and stand up for the rights of children and young people with disabilities, autism and additional needs.

We work with families, for families.We listen to their voices, learn from their lived experiences and turn their needs into action. We will continue to stand up, speak out and advocate for what is right.

  1. 01

    Partnership

    Stronger Together

    Families are at the heart of everything we do. We bring together children, young people, parents, carers, volunteers, professionals and communities as equal partners. By listening, respecting and working together, we create stronger families and better futures.

  2. 02

    Support

    No Family Stands Alone

    We provide compassionate, practical and meaningful support when families need it most. We create a community where families feel welcomed, understood and valued.

    We don’t just provide support — we walk alongside families through every challenge and every achievement.

  3. 03

    Empowerment

    Turning Voices into Strength

    We empower children, young people, parents and carers to recognise their strengths, build confidence and have a powerful voice in the decisions that affect their lives.

    Every voice matters. Every ability has value. Every person has potential.

  4. 04

    Opportunity

    Opening Doors. Removing Barriers.

    We believe disability should never limit dreams or opportunities. We work to break down barriers and create inclusive opportunities for children and young people to learn, develop, build friendships, gain independence and discover what they can achieve.

    We focus not on limitations, but on possibilities and potential.

  5. 05

    Advocacy & Awareness

    Speaking Up and Changing Lives.

    We advocate fearlessly for children, young people and families whose voices are too often unheard.

    Through education, awareness and community engagement, we challenge stigma and increase understanding of autism, learning disabilities and additional needs.

    We speak up when families are not heard, challenge inequality and work towards a society where every child is respected, included and valued.

One Belief. One Voice. One Promise.

No child overlooked. No voice unheard. No family left behind.

How Our Service Works

Early support can change a child’s journey and strengthen an entire family.

We listen without judgement, support with compassion and empower families to move forward with confidence.

Acceptance & Understanding

From uncertainty to understanding. From understanding to action.

We help families understand and embrace their child’s individual needs, challenge stigma and access the right support. Early acceptance opens the door to earlier support and greater opportunities.

Early Intervention

The Right Support. At the Right Time.

Every child deserves the best possible start in life. We help families access early support, information and opportunities that can strengthen children’s development, confidence, independence and wellbeing.

Financial & Practical Support

Because No Family Should Struggle Alone.

Caring for a child with additional needs can bring emotional, practical and financial pressures. We provide information, advocacy, practical assistance and signposting to help families access the support they are entitled to.

Our Promise

We meet families where they are, stand beside them through the challenges and help them move forward with hope.

Accept. Support. Empower. Thrive.
