About Us

An award winning charity

Dimobi is a special needs and learning disability, autism provider based in Manchester.

We work closely with families, for families. Everything we do is carried out in the hope of improving the lives of our families. We achieve this by working directly with parents and carers. We are driven by what they tell us.

Our mission

To help families accept and support children, young people and adults up to 25 years of age with learning disabilities and/or autism.

Our aims

To Equip Parents with the Extra Skills and Confidence Needed to Nurture and Develop Disabled Children. To Enable Disabled Children to Have a Happy Childhood

What we Believe

Our Values. Our Strength Our Commitment.

We believe every child and young person deserves the opportunity to be seen, heard, included and empowered to thrive.

We are ambitious for our families because they deserve nothing less. We challenge barriers, champion inclusion and stand up for the rights of children and young people with disabilities, autism and additional needs.

We work with families, for families.We listen to their voices, learn from their lived experiences and turn their needs into action. We will continue to stand up, speak out and advocate for what is right.

  1. 01

    Partnership

    Stronger Together

    Families are at the heart of everything we do. We bring together children, young people, parents, carers, volunteers, professionals and communities as equal partners. By listening, respecting and working together, we create stronger families and better futures.

  2. 02

    Support

    No Family Stands Alone

    We provide compassionate, practical and meaningful support when families need it most. We create a community where families feel welcomed, understood and valued.

    We don’t just provide support — we walk alongside families through every challenge and every achievement.

  3. 03

    Empowerment

    Turning Voices into Strength

    We empower children, young people, parents and carers to recognise their strengths, build confidence and have a powerful voice in the decisions that affect their lives.

    Every voice matters. Every ability has value. Every person has potential.

  4. 04

    Opportunity

    Opening Doors. Removing Barriers.

    We believe disability should never limit dreams or opportunities. We work to break down barriers and create inclusive opportunities for children and young people to learn, develop, build friendships, gain independence and discover what they can achieve.

    We focus not on limitations, but on possibilities and potential.

  5. 05

    Advocacy & Awareness

    Speaking Up and Changing Lives.

    We advocate fearlessly for children, young people and families whose voices are too often unheard.

    Through education, awareness and community engagement, we challenge stigma and increase understanding of autism, learning disabilities and additional needs.

    We speak up when families are not heard, challenge inequality and work towards a society where every child is respected, included and valued.

One Belief. One Voice. One Promise.

No child overlooked. No voice unheard. No family left behind.

Our Programmes

Support That Changes Lives

We provide inclusive, practical and compassionate support for children and young people with disabilities, autism, learning disabilities and additional needs, as well as their parents, carers and wider families.

We don’t believe families should have to face challenges alone. From the first conversation to ongoing advocacy and practical support, we are here to listen, empower, advocate and walk alongside families.

Autism & Disability Support

Understanding Needs. Unlocking Potential.

We provide advice, information and personalised support to children and young people with autism, learning disabilities and additional needs. We also support parents, carers and professionals to better understand individual needs and access appropriate services.

Children & Young People’s Activities

Every Child Deserves to Belong.

Our inclusive holiday programmes, wellbeing activities, trips and community events give children and young people opportunities to have fun, build friendships, develop confidence and learn new skills.

Activities can include arts and crafts, music, dance, games, storytelling, creative activities, social activities and community experiences.

Parents’ & Carers’ Forum

Your Voice. Your Community. Your Strength.

Our Parents’ and Carers’ Forum provides a welcoming peer-to-peer network where families can connect, share experiences and access information, workshops and support.

We empower parents and carers to have a stronger voice in decisions affecting their children and encourage co-production, ensuring services are shaped with families rather than simply for them.

Family Support & Advocacy

Standing With Families When It Matters Most.

We provide one-to-one support and advocacy to help families navigate difficult situations and access the services and opportunities they need.

Our support includes form filling, letter writing, mediation, signposting, brief interventions, court support, information about benefits and finances, and advocacy with services and professionals.

Carers’ Wellbeing

Caring for Those Who Care for Others.

Carers give so much every day. We provide opportunities for carers to focus on their own emotional, social and physical wellbeing.

Our support includes carers’ lunches, drop-in sessions, peer support, wellbeing activities, social opportunities, training sessions and occasional pampering and relaxation activities.

Community Workshops & Training

Knowledge Builds Confidence.

We deliver disability and culturally responsive awareness sessions, workshops and training for parents, carers, volunteers, community members and professionals.

Our workshops help increase understanding of autism, disability and additional needs while giving families practical knowledge and confidence to advocate for themselves and their children.

Advice, Information & Signposting

Helping Families Find the Right Support.

Finding the right service can sometimes feel overwhelming. We provide accessible advice and information and connect families with appropriate local services, specialist organisations and other sources of support.

Domestic Abuse Support

A Safe Space to Be Heard and Supported.

Through our specialist family support, we provide confidential and compassionate assistance to carers affected by domestic abuse.

We listen without judgement, provide emotional and practical support, advocate where appropriate and signpost to specialist services when additional help is required.

Food Relief & Practical Support

Supporting Families Through Difficult Times.

Through Dimobi Food Relief Support, we provide food parcels and practical assistance to families experiencing financial hardship or crisis.

Our aim is not only to respond to immediate need but also to help families access wider support that can improve their longer-term circumstances.

Youth Voice & Participation

Nothing About Young People Without Young People.

We create opportunities for young people to express their views, develop confidence and participate in decisions that affect them.

We believe young people’s experiences and ideas should help shape the programmes and services designed for them.

Peer Buddy Support

Connection. Friendship. Belonging.

Our peer support approach brings people together through shared experiences, helping children, young people, parents and carers build supportive relationships and reduce isolation.

Co-Production

Designed With Families, Not Just For Families.

Families are experts through experience. Our Co-Production Group creates opportunities for parents, carers and young people to work alongside professionals and organisations to influence and improve services.

Volunteering

Give Your Time. Share Your Skills. Change a Life.

We provide meaningful volunteering opportunities for people who want to contribute their experience, skills and compassion to supporting children, young people and families.

Parents’ & Carers’ Forum

Together We Are Stronger

Our Parents’ and Carers’ Forum is a supportive space where families can connect, learn, share and have their voices heard.

The Forum offers:

  • Peer-to-peer support
  • Parent and carer workshops
  • Advice and information
  • Emotional and wellbeing support
  • Youth voice and participation
  • Co-production opportunities
  • Advocacy and signposting
  • Training and awareness sessions
  • Social activities and opportunities to connect with other families

You don’t have to navigate the journey alone. At Dimobi, you belong to a community that listens, understands and stands beside you.

At Dimobi Children Disability Trust, we don’t just provide services — we build stronger families, create opportunities and help children and young people thrive.

How Our Service Works

Early support can change a child’s journey and strengthen an entire family.

We listen without judgement, support with compassion and empower families to move forward with confidence.

Acceptance & Understanding

From uncertainty to understanding. From understanding to action.

We help families understand and embrace their child’s individual needs, challenge stigma and access the right support. Early acceptance opens the door to earlier support and greater opportunities.

Early Intervention

The Right Support. At the Right Time.

Every child deserves the best possible start in life. We help families access early support, information and opportunities that can strengthen children’s development, confidence, independence and wellbeing.

Financial & Practical Support

Because No Family Should Struggle Alone.

Caring for a child with additional needs can bring emotional, practical and financial pressures. We provide information, advocacy, practical assistance and signposting to help families access the support they are entitled to.

Our Promise

We meet families where they are, stand beside them through the challenges and help them move forward with hope.

Accept. Support. Empower. Thrive.
