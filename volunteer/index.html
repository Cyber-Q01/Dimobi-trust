Volunteer With Us

Volunteer with Us.

We always have projects on the go which need enthusiastic helpers. Volunteer with Dimobi and make a real difference to childrens’ lives.

There are a number of ways you can help us

We need your help

Would you like to help us? Can you spare some time?

Offer your time

You could offer your time – most of our volunteers commit approximately 4 hours a week to support a family.

2-4 hours / week
Location

Food parcels
